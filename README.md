# Blackest (preview of And-Roids)

One tap to make your phone as black as it gets **without root**, and one tap to undo it.

## What it does
- Turns dark theme on.
- Turns apps that have no dark mode **true black** (smart invert; pictures and videos stay normal).
- Sets the darkest system color palette Android can build (style SPRITZ, seed black), where the phone supports it:
  Android 17 theme service, or the Pixel-style theme setting on Android 16 and older. Phones that build their colors
  another way (for example Samsung One UI) skip this step, and the app says so.
- Turns on Extra Dim (level 60), turns off window blur, dims the wallpaper.

Before its first change it saves every original value. **Restore** puts each one back exactly.

## What it can't do
Make every system screen pure black. Settings and other system screens go near-black; pure black there needs root.

## Needs
- Android 11 or newer (best on Android 16 and 17 with a Pixel-style system).
- [Shizuku](https://github.com/RikkaApps/Shizuku) running (Shizuku or Shizuku-Next), and Blackest allowed in it.

## Use
1. Start Shizuku. 2. Open Blackest, accept the notice, tap **Connect to Shizuku** and Allow.
3. Tap **Go black**. Tap **Restore** to undo. Or add the **Blackest** Quick Settings tile and toggle it from there. **Send a report** shares a plain-text report with the app you pick.
